# Jayden — jayden-portfolio.framer.website

**Positioning:** one-page personal portfolio (by Ridhwan), "modern, minimal,
portfolio-focused," with dedicated case-study depth: projects, experience,
skills, services, achievements, testimonials, contact in a single flow.

## Palette & type
- Minimal and memorable; portfolio imagery is the color, chrome stays neutral
  *(reported "clean visual style")*.

## Layout & rhythm
- **The one-page arc done well**: every classic portfolio section stacked in a
  single scroll without feeling endless *(reported section list)*.
- Experience/achievements presented as compact rows with dates — résumé data
  as design element *(typical of this template)*.

## Motion & interaction
- Smooth scroll-linked reveals per section; "smooth animations [that] ensure
  your portfolio showcase remains the centerpiece" *(reported)*.

## What we take
- Confidence that a single page can carry experience + skills + testimonials
  without pagination — pacing handled by alternating dense/airy sections.
- Compact experience rows (role, company, dates) inside our About section.

## What we skip
- Separate case-study pages; our project detail lives in expanded work cards.
