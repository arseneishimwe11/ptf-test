# ClearPath — clearpath-template.framer.website

**Positioning:** $99 therapy/coaching template — "calming, trustworthy," "soft
colors and smooth interactions that foster a sense of calm and trust." Included
in the reference set as the **counter-reference**: the light, soft end of the
spectrum.

## Palette & type
- Soft, warm, low-contrast palette; rounded shapes; airy sections *(reported)*.

## Layout & rhythm
- Slow pacing, few items per viewport, generous line-height — designed to lower
  arousal rather than impress *(reported tone)*.

## Motion & interaction
- Gentle fades only; motion never startles *(reported "smooth interactions…
  calm")*.

## What we take
- The pacing lesson for our **reduced-motion fallback**: when
  `prefers-reduced-motion` is on, the site should degrade to ClearPath-like
  calm (opacity-only transitions, no parallax, static hero) and still feel
  designed, not broken.
- Warm-tinted neutrals: our dark ground is warmed slightly (not pure #000) so
  long reading stays comfortable.

## What we skip
- Light theme, wellness tone, booking machinery.
