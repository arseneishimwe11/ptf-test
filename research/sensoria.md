# Sensoria — sensoria.framer.website

**Positioning:** free AI beauty/skin-analysis template by Liana Tudakova. The
"immersive dark sci-fi" reference: "sterile, futuristic aesthetic," "neural-
aesthetic motion," "dark immersive" visual system.

## Palette & type
- Deep dark ground with clinical, high-contrast light text and instrument-like
  micro-labels; premium-AI-product feel *(reported)*.

## Layout & rhythm
- Full-screen video hero with animated headline *(reported)*.
- **Stats block with live metrics** ("scan accuracy, monitoring hours, sync
  speed") *(reported)* — numbers presented as instrument readouts.
- Comparison/progress modules; semantic accessibility tags noted in listing.

## Motion & interaction
- Animated hero headline over ambient full-screen motion *(reported)*.
- "Neural-aesthetic" motion: fine lines, scan/g rid overlays, precision easing
  *(reported branding, typical execution)*.

## What we take
- Instrument-readout stats: our hero/about metrics animate as count-ups with
  tabular numerals and hairline rules.
- Ambient full-viewport hero motion — ours is an original GLSL flow-field
  shader (no video asset dependency), dimmed and pausable, reduced-motion-safe.
- Fine hairline rules + uppercase micro-labels as a recurring texture.

## What we skip
- Clinical/beauty tone; comparison sliders.
