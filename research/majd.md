# Majd — majd-portfolio.framer.website

**Positioning:** free personal-portfolio template by Mejed Alkoutaini (software engineer,
Syria). Widely recommended as the "clean structure, smooth flow" baseline for
developer portfolios. Pages: Home, Work (CMS), Project (CMS), 404.

## Palette & type
- Minimal, high-contrast neutral scheme; attention deliberately kept on project
  imagery rather than chrome *(reported)*.
- Single grotesk family across weights; hierarchy achieved with size/weight, not
  extra families *(typical)*.

## Layout & rhythm
- Narrow content column, generous vertical whitespace between sections *(reported:
  "clean structure… minimal aesthetic that keeps attention on projects")*.
- Work list as full-width rows rather than dense grid *(typical for this genre)*.

## Motion & interaction
- **Lenis smooth scrolling** (darkroom.engineering) — explicitly credited in the
  listing. The inertia scroll is the template's signature feel.
- Motion intentionally light-touch *(reported: "without fighting busy defaults
  that distract from headline clarity")* — fade/slide reveals on scroll, no heavy
  parallax or WebGL.
- Hover states on work rows: image reveal or subtle shift *(typical)*.

## What we take
- Lenis as the scroll backbone — the single highest-leverage "feel" upgrade.
- The restraint lesson: reveals serve reading order; nothing animates twice.
- Full-width work rows as one of two candidate work-grid layouts.

## What we skip
- Multi-page CMS structure — ours is a single-page scroll.
