# Hanza — hanza-template.framer.website

**Positioning:** modern portfolio & agency template for designers, developers,
freelancers, studios. Sells on structure: "page structure, user flow, and key
sections… planned around clarity, hierarchy, and purpose."

## Palette & type
- Contemporary neutral scheme with strong type hierarchy; clean rather than
  moody *(reported "clean structure and smooth motion")*.

## Layout & rhythm
- Comprehensive section inventory: selected work, services, case studies,
  testimonials, pricing, blog, contact *(reported)* — the most complete
  information architecture among the references.
- Sections built on a consistent grid so blocks can be re-ordered without
  breaking rhythm *(reported emphasis on customization)*.

## Motion & interaction
- "Smooth motion with a focus on usability" *(reported)* — scroll reveals,
  hover elevation on cards, no attention-competing effects.

## What we take
- The IA lesson: a single page still needs the full narrative arc —
  intro → proof (work) → capability (services/skills) → trust (testimonials)
  → action (contact). Our section order mirrors that arc.
- Consistent card grammar across sections (same radius/spacing family for work
  cards, testimonial cards, skill chips).

## What we skip
- Pricing and blog sections — irrelevant to a personal engineer portfolio.
