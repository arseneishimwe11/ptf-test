# Platform® — plat-form.framer.ai

**Positioning:** premium modular landing template (by Tamas) for SaaS/AI/digital
products. Marketed on "every section earns its place," bento-style modularity,
and a "dark, editorial" system that "looks like it cost 10x more to build."

## Palette & type
- **Dark editorial system**: near-black ground, refined typography, editorial
  spacing, fluid micro-interactions, video-background hero *(all reported)*.
- The strongest palette/typography reference for the "expensive dark" look.

## Layout & rhythm
- **Bento grid** blocks that can be moved/swapped without breaking the layout
  *(reported)* — cards share a strict radius/gap system.
- Conversion-first order: Hero → Work → Services → Process → Analytics →
  Pricing → Testimonials → Contact *(reported)*.

## Motion & interaction
- Fluid micro-interactions (hover states, magnetic-feeling buttons), background
  video for ambient motion *(reported)*.

## What we take
- The dark editorial token system: one ground color family, one ink family,
  one accent; strict radius scale; generous letter-spaced micro-labels
  (section eyebrows) — the fastest route to "studio-built" feel.
- Bento grammar for our Skills/stack section (mixed-size tiles, shared gap).
- Ambient hero motion — but shader/WebGL instead of video (lighter, original,
  and content-free until real assets exist).

## What we skip
- Pricing/analytics blocks; product-marketing tone.
