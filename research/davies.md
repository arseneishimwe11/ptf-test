# Davies — davies.framer.website

**Positioning:** sleek one-page personal portfolio "crafted for speed,
simplicity, and impact" by a designer/no-code developer known for bold,
award-style work.

## Palette & type
- Bold-simple: very large display type against quiet ground; personality via
  scale, not decoration *(reported "bold, functional")*.

## Layout & rhythm
- "Well-structured one-page layout… smooth animations and balanced spacing"
  *(reported)* — the spacing benchmark among the one-pagers. Sections breathe;
  nothing is cramped to fit above the fold.
- Intro → projects → skills/experience → contact, each with one clear job
  *(reported section list)*.

## Motion & interaction
- Seamless scroll from section to section; reveals timed to reading order
  *(reported "scroll seamlessly through… with smooth animations")*.

## What we take
- The spacing courage: section paddings in the 8–12rem class on desktop, type
  scale that jumps (not creeps) between levels.
- One-job-per-section discipline to keep the long page scannable.

## What we skip
- Nothing structural — Davies is closest in spirit; we go more cinematic on
  top of its skeleton.
